# Beanstalk — New Tester Quick Start

Welcome! This walks you from your TestFlight invite to placing your first
trade in a contest. It takes about 5 minutes. 🌱

---

## 1. Accept the TestFlight invite (one time)

1. Check the email inbox for the address you gave us — the invite comes from
   **App Store Connect** with the subject **"You're invited to test Beanstalk."**
   *(If you got a public link instead, just tap it on your iPhone and skip to
   step 3.)*
2. On your **iPhone**, install **TestFlight** from the App Store if you don't
   already have it (it's Apple's free app for testing pre-release apps).
3. Open the invite email on the phone and tap **View in TestFlight** →
   **Accept** → **Install**.
4. Beanstalk now appears on your home screen with an **orange dot** next to the
   name (that dot just means "TestFlight build" — normal).

> Builds expire after 90 days. When a new version is ready, TestFlight will
> prompt you to update — please do, so you're testing the latest.

---

## 2. Create your account

1. Open **Beanstalk**.
2. On the login screen tap **Sign Up** (bottom of the screen).
3. Enter your **email** and a **password**, then submit.
   - Login is by email — there's no separate username.
   - Use a password you'll remember. If you forget it later, tap
     **Forgot password?** on the login screen to reset it by email.

---

## 3. Onboard (5 quick screens)

After signing up you'll go through a short intro:

1. **Welcome** — tap through.
2. **Who are you?** — pick your age group (High School / College / Young
   Professional / Adult). *This is required — contests use it to place you in
   the right group.*
3. **Risk quiz** — a couple of taps.
4. **Results** — your investor profile.
5. **Your name** — enter a display name and finish.

You'll land on your **main portfolio** with a starting balance of **$10,000**
in play money.

---

## 4. Join a contest

1. Tap the **Contests** tab.
2. Pick a contest from the list to open it.
3. Tap **Join Contest** (the button shows the current player count, e.g.
   *Join Contest · 3/50*).
4. You'll see a **"Joined! Good luck 🌱"** confirmation, and the button changes
   to **You're in · N players**.

---

## 5. Trade inside the contest

Each contest is its own separate workspace — a contest portfolio, kept apart
from your main portfolio, with its own starting balance and holdings.

1. In the open contest, use the tabs across the top:
   **Details · Portfolio · Standings · Chat**.
2. Tap **Portfolio** to see your contest cash and holdings.
3. Tap **Trade in this contest** (or the trade button) to buy your first stock.
4. Tap **Standings** any time to see where you rank against other players.

That's it — you're live. Trade, climb the standings, and see if you can win. 🏆
